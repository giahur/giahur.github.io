Pizza Saucer

2-D pizza-making simulator game

Role: Producer, Gameplay and UI Developer
Team Size: 5 people
Duration: 3 months
Unity C#

You've been wandering the galaxy for months with no luck. With your navigator down, you’ve flown into countless asteroid fields and space wars. You don't know the way home... but you know how to make pizza. Satisfy your alien customers, discover new ingredients and recipes, and find your way back home!

Process

Cooking Mechanic

As gameplay developer, I worked on the pizza mechanic. This included adding toppings to the pizza, cooking it in the oven (with pizza state transitions), and serving it to the customer. After playtests, we received feedback that the drag-and-drop mechanic was tedious, so this was switched to clicking on the topping bin and placing the toppings until right-click.

Recipe Book System

Additionally, one of my main focuses was the recipe book UI. Players could look through the recipe book to see descriptions of each ingredient in order to find which one a customer might prefer. When players combine ingredients, the customer may return and inform the player about the new flavor profile that this creates. The recipe book will show a red dot to indicate that new information has been added, and the unique combination is programmatically added to the book.
