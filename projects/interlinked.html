Interlinked

3-D First-Person Action Game

Unreal Engine 5 C# Blueprints GAS
Role: Developer
Team Size: 13 people
Duration: 4 months

Play as a divine soldier. Armed with six spells themed around flesh and technology, kill enemies to harvest enhancements.

Responsibilities

Programmer

  • Worked with three other developers on the development subteam. Contributed to systems architecture, AI programming, and technical documentation.
  • Spells and System Architecture
    • Researched and evaluated approaches for the spell system, designing an initial architecture with a SpellManager, SpellBase, and individual spell managers.
    • Researched and integrated Unreal Engine 5's Gameplay Ability System (GAS), replacing spell architecture. This reduced implementation overhead and provided built-in support for damage effects and gameplay cues.
    • Wrote internal documention on GAS to onboard the rest of the development team.
    • Established and documented project-wide conventions: file structure, naming conventions, and coding guidelines.
    • Implemented the Flesh Wall spell: a placeable wall that expires after a set duration.
  • Enemy AI
    • Reviewed design team's Meatloaf behavior specification and translated it into an Unreal Engine Behavior Tree.
    • Implemented Meatloaf's solo behaviors: patrolling, player detection, movement, and projectile attack.
    • Researched AI formation behavior, writing documentation for the rest of the development team's reference.
    • Implemented Meatloaf formation behavior: multiple Meatloafs join side-by-side, walking and attacking in formation and restructuring when needed.
    • Assisted in implementing physical materials to give enemy's targeted damage zones.
    • Researched and implemented procedural animation using Unreal's locomotion plugin.

Process

Interlinked is a 3D first-person action game built in Unreal Engine 5. This project was built across three milestones as part of a graduate-level game production course. Players could cast six spells including Digitize, Volt Cascade, Life Dash, Contagion, Flesh Wall, and Lockjaw. Levels were populated with three enemy archetypes: CCG, Torture Nexus, and Meatloaf. The fnal deliverable was a playable demo that served as a tutorial level, introducing the spells, enemies, and enhancement system.
Milestone 1: Core Loop and Scoping. The team entered this course with a very ambitious scope. We had initially planned for six enemies, six spells, and multiple levels. After discussion with the 3D artists, this was cut down to 3 enemies. The development team was able to implement all six spells in this milestone, setting up the architecture with the GAS framework. This included gameplay tags, gameplay effects, gameplay cues, and gameplay attributes.
Milestone 2: AI Solo Behaviors and Playtest Feedback. In this milestone, we began conducting playtests and implementing feedback. This included spell onboarding, pacing, level design, and UI issues. In this milestone, the development team pivoted to implementing the AI enemies' behavior trees based on the design team's descriptions.
Milestone 3: AI Group Behaviors, Polish, and Integration. In the final milestone, the team focused on polishing the game through whiteboxing and bug fixes. The development team continued to focus on AI behavior trees, now focusing on group behavior. This meant formations within enemy factions, as well as enemy affiliations with each other. We also implemented all models and animations for the player, spells, and enemies.
